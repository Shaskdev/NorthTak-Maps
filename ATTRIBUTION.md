# Data sources and attribution

NorthTak Maps is an unofficial, automatically generated set of map overlays. It
is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any of the organizations below. Data may
be delayed, incomplete or wrong; always confirm with the official source.

| Layer | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Alberta Wildfires | Alberta Wildfire, Government of Alberta, *Alberta Fire Status* map service (`geospatial.alberta.ca`) | [Open Government Licence – Alberta](https://open.alberta.ca/licence) |
| Satellite Fire Perimeters | Canadian Forest Service. Canadian Wildland Fire Information System (CWFIS), Natural Resources Canada, Canadian Forest Service, Northern Forestry Centre, Edmonton, Alberta. https://cwfis.cfs.nrcan.gc.ca | [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada) |
| Weather Alerts | Data Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada (MSC GeoMet `weather-alerts`). Alert text is reproduced unaltered. | [ECCC Data Services End-use Licence](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/licence/readme_en/) |
| Earthquakes | Earthquakes Canada, Natural Resources Canada (FDSN event service) | [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada) |
| Health Facilities | Statistics Canada, *Open Database of Healthcare Facilities* (ODHF) v1.1, 2020 | [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada) |
| Alberta 511 Road Events | Alberta 511 via Esri Canada `Alberta511` feature service | **Not confirmed** – built only with `--include-unconfirmed`, never published |
